(The 3AM Fridge Text, $2 winner-take-all, deadline 25 Nov 2026 20:45 WIB)

Judge criteria, most important first: (1) a one-line text a smart fridge
sends its owner at 3am, and funny; (2) the bigger laugh — the line a reader
would screenshot and send to a friend; (3) sounds like a fridge: a machine
with a small voice, an opinion, and no shame about sharing it at 3am;
(4) works as one line of SMS, short enough for a lock screen, complete
without a follow-up; (5) a fresh angle — NOT milk, ice, or a door left open.

## Notes per candidate

1. **Rank 1 — "awake since 2019" (104).** (1) unmistakably a fridge texting
   at 3am; (2) the payoff "One of us needs a hobby, and it is not me"
   lands last and reframes the whole line — the machine roasting the human
   for being awake; (3) pure machine voice: it measures wakefulness in
   years, the owner in minutes; (4) one sentence chain, complete, no
   follow-up needed; (5) fresh — the angle is the fridge's eternal
   wakefulness, not food, cold, or a door.
2. **Rank 2 — firmware update (99).** Strong machine voice and a great
   closer ("We need to talk"), "I have notes" is very screenshot-able —
   but "judge everything you eat" leans on the eating-at-3am trope, so the
   setup is less fresh than #1.
3. **Rank 3 — only appliance working (126).** Fresh angle (appliance labour,
   the dishwasher quitting at nine) and a clear small voice — but the laugh
   is a wry smile rather than a screenshot, and it is the second-longest
   line without earning the length.
4. **Rank 4 — not a television (115).** "I am a fridge, not a television"
   is a sharp punch, but the setup (owner opening the fridge to stare) is
   the most familiar 3am-fridge image in the field, so freshness is weak.
5. **Rank 5 — unlike you (114).** Good twist ending ("Unlike you") and a
   machine that hears the owner sneaking — but "one light, no secrets"
   needs a beat to parse, so it reads slightly slower on a lock screen.
6. **Rank 6 — same leftovers (128).** The repetition payoff is solid, but
   leftovers-at-3am is a well-worn setup and the line is long for its laugh.
7. **Rank 7 — raccoon (123).** The raccoon image is vivid and funny, but
   "staring into my light" is again the familiar staring setup, and the
   question form softens the punch.
8. **Rank 8 — keeping a log (91).** Shortest, and the blackmail-log angle is
   fresh — but the "— Your Fridge" sign-off spends characters stating what
   the voice already makes obvious, and the laugh is thinner than #1's.

## Verdict

**Winner: #1** — "I have been awake since 2019. You have been awake since
2:47. One of us needs a hobby, and it is not me." (104 chars)

Pairwise it beats every other candidate on criterion 5 (the eternal-
wakefulness angle is the freshest in the set) while matching or beating them
on 2–3: the payoff lands in the last six words and the whole line is the
machine deciding, at 3am, that it has finally had enough of being the only
one awake. Runner-up: #2 (strong closer, weaker on freshness).
